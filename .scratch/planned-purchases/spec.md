# Planned purchases

## Problem Statement

The household can see where money went and where the year is heading, but not when it can afford the next thing it wants to buy. Deciding "can we get the new laptop in spring?" today means eyeballing income, the recurring bills, what a contract still owes, and the monthly Goal in your head.

## Solution

A short-term planning page, **Acquisti programmati**. The household keeps a priority-ordered list of **Planned purchases** (label, amount, optional Category). The app projects the **Headroom** of each of the next 6 months (starting next month) and places each Planned purchase, in priority order, in the first month whose accumulated Headroom covers it. One that fits in none of the 6 months is not a short-term purchase: the page says how much is missing and whether Savings would cover that gap or a loan would be needed. Buying one turns it into an Expense.

See CONTEXT.md: **Planned purchase**, **Headroom**.

## User Stories

**The list**

1. As a household member, I want to add a Planned purchase with a label, an amount, and optionally a Category, so I can write down what I intend to buy.
2. As a household member, I want a new Planned purchase to go to the bottom of the list, so adding one never reshuffles my priorities.
3. As a household member, I want to move a Planned purchase up or down with one tap, so the order says what matters most.
4. As a household member, I want to edit or delete a Planned purchase through the same actions sidebar as every other record, so there is nothing new to learn.

**Headroom**

5. As a household member, I want to see, for each of the next 6 months, the projected Headroom and the running total, so I understand why a purchase lands where it does.
6. As a household member, I want a Contract that ends, or a Recurring expense that stops, to show up in the right month, so the projection follows what I already know about the future.
7. As a household member, I want Investments to count only as money leaving, never as money to spend, so a PAC contribution never looks like spare cash.
8. As a household member, I want a month with negative Headroom to eat into what earlier months accumulated, because that is what happens to the account.
9. As a household member, I want the Headroom rows labeled as a projection, so I never mistake them for facts.
10. As a household member with less than 3 months of history, I want to be told the projection needs more history, while still being able to save Planned purchases.

**Placement**

11. As a household member, I want each Planned purchase to show the month it fits in, in priority order, with each placed purchase using up the Headroom it needs.
12. As a household member, I want a Planned purchase that doesn't fit within 6 months to be skipped without blocking the smaller ones after it.
13. As a household member, I want a Planned purchase that doesn't fit to show how much is missing and whether my Savings cover that gap, so I know whether to dip into Savings or look for a loan.

**Buying**

14. As a household member, I want a "Comprato" action that opens the Expense form prefilled with the amount, Category and label, so turning a plan into an Expense is one tap.
15. As a household member, I want the Planned purchase to leave the list once that Expense is saved, and stay if I cancel.

## Implementation Decisions

- **Horizon:** fixed at 6 months, starting the month after the current one. The current month is never offered: its leftover is still being spent.
- **Headroom of a future month M** = typical Income + Contract share(M) − typical non-recurring spending − Recurring expenses due in M − Goal.
  - *Typical Income*: median of the trailing completed months' received Income (by payment date) that references no Contract.
  - *Contract share(M)*: for each Contract active in M, what it still owes (total − received) ÷ months left until its end month, from next month on. A Contract past its end month contributes nothing.
  - *Typical non-recurring spending*: median of the trailing completed months' Expenses not generated by a Recurring expense, **including** Investments.
  - *Recurring expenses due in M*: every Recurring expense running in M (start month ≤ M, no end month or end month ≥ M), including Investments ones.
  - *Goal*: the household's monthly savings Goal.
  - Medians reuse Budget's history rule: trailing 12 completed months, only months since the first Expense, at least 3 of them. Otherwise the projection is unavailable.
- **Placement:** walk the list in priority order carrying the running Headroom balance; a purchase lands in the first month whose balance (after higher-priority purchases) covers its amount, and that amount leaves the balance from that month on. One that fits in no month consumes nothing. Its missing amount = its amount − the balance at the end of month 6 (after higher-priority placements, floored at 0 balance); Savings cover it when Savings ≥ missing amount.
- Current Savings never enter the Headroom calculation (only the "does Savings cover the gap" answer).
- **Reordering:** up/down controls, no drag and drop, no new dependency.

## Schema

`schemaVersion` 22 → 23, one new table:

```sql
CREATE TABLE planned_purchase (
  id           INTEGER PRIMARY KEY,
  label        TEXT    NOT NULL,
  amount_cents INTEGER NOT NULL CHECK (amount_cents > 0),
  category_id  INTEGER REFERENCES category(id),
  position     INTEGER NOT NULL
);
```

`position` is the priority: lower first. A new row gets max(position) + 1.

## Testing Decisions

- Backend tests for the Headroom projection with a fixed clock: income median excluding Contract Incomes, Contract share ending mid-horizon, non-recurring median excluding generated Expenses, a Recurring expense with an end month inside the horizon, Investments as outflow, a negative month, under-3-months unavailable.
- Backend tests for placement: priority order, skip-without-blocking, missing amount, Savings coverage.
- Backend tests for create/update/delete/reorder round-trips.

## Out of Scope

- Investment performance (no price history exists).
- Prices, sales, seasons.
- A "by when" date, a Dashboard card, a configurable horizon.
- Recurring incomes (still "da valutare" in TODO.md).
