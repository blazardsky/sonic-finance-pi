# Income records what arrived; tax is an Expense when paid

An Income stores one number: the amount that hit the account. There is no gross/net pair and no tax-percentage field, even though this household's income is largely freelance.

Taxes are recorded as ordinary Expenses on the day they are paid. Netting the Income *as well* would deduct the same tax twice — once as a smaller income, once as an expense. One number in, one number out, no double-count and no forecasting machinery.
