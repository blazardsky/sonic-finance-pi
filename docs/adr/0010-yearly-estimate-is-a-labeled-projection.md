# The yearly Estimate is the app's first projected figure, and is always labeled as such

Every other number in this app is a cash-basis fact (ADR-0003, ADR-0004): Income counts only when received, tax is an ordinary Expense. The Dashboard's yearly Estimate breaks that pattern deliberately — it projects income, expenses, and tax for the rest of the year by applying this year's year-to-date growth (against last year's YTD at the same point, in whole completed months) to last year's full-year totals.

Because it is the only speculative number the app shows, it replaces the Dashboard's previous fixed progress-bar targets and must always be visually and textually distinguished from actuals, so it is never mistaken for money that has actually moved.

## Consequences

A household with less than a year of history sees no Estimate — there is nothing to compute a year-over-year ratio from.
