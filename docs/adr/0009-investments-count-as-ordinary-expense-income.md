# Investments stay in ordinary totals; only Budget, Target, and the Estimate exclude them

Buying or selling a Holding is recorded as an ordinary Expense or Income under the protected `Investments` base category — not a separate ledger. It counts in Month, Year, Dashboard, and Recent totals exactly like any other Expense or Income; only the new Budget/Target (median monthly spending) and the yearly Estimate exclude it, because those two exist specifically to answer "am I on track with ordinary living costs," and a lumpy stock purchase would make that meaningless.

The alternative — excluding Investments everywhere — would hide that the money genuinely left the household and might not come back if the market falls, and would require adding a filter to every existing report query (Month, Year, Recent, the daily/weekly chart) rather than only the two reports built from scratch for this.

## Consequences

A month with a large stock purchase shows a correspondingly large Expense total and a negative Net, honestly reflecting cash flow — expected, not a bug.
